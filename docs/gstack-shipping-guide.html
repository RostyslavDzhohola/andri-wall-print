How to continue shipping with GStack

You do not need to finish the whole Office Hours vision in one pass. GStack works best when you turn the big direction into a sequence of shippable slices.

The mental model

Office Hours Defines the problem, user, wedge, and what should not be built yet.
CEO Review Chooses what to expand, hold, or defer for the product direction.
Plan Engineer Turns a chosen slice into architecture, tests, edge cases, and tasks.
Build + QA Implements the slice and proves current behavior with tests and browser checks.
Ship Reviews the actual diff, audits plan completion, updates docs, and creates the PR.

When to rerun each GStack skill

Rerun Office Hours

  • The product direction changes.
  • Seller observation invalidates the wedge.
  • You are deciding what this should become.

Rerun CEO Review

  • You want to add major scope.
  • You are debating payment, booking, pricing, native app, or platform direction.
  • The plan feels too small or too broad.

Rerun Plan Engineer

  • The architecture changes.
  • The branch-local review gate needs a clean current-commit record.
  • You are starting a new complex slice.
