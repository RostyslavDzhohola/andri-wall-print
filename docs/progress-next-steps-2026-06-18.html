What We Agreed To Build

A client-facing sales pilot for Wall Print Pro. Prospects can see wall-print ideas, submit request/reserve interest, optionally generate a concept draft, and give the seller enough context to follow up. Final quote, measurement, scheduling, and payment stay seller-managed.

Do not confuse this with

  • A full measurement/payment platform.
  • A public quote calculator.
  • A generic SaaS dashboard.
  • A final-print guarantee for AI concepts.

Plan Decisions

  • Direction: sales pilot, not full platform.
  • Lead intake: add public no-auth request flow.
  • AI: add contact-gated concept drafts now.
  • Admin: add simple seller lead inbox.
  • Accounts: optional for prospects, not a blocker.
  • Payments: real booking and deposits deferred.
